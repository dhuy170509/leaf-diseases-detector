## New Sections Explained

### 👨‍⚕️ Expert Explanation
**What it is**: Professional description of the disease
- What causes it
- How it affects the plant
- Why it's important to treat

**Example**: "Đốm lá là bệnh phổ biến gây ra bởi các loại nấm..."

### 💡 Professional Recommendations  
**What to do**: 4 specific, actionable recommendations
- Immediate actions
- Treatment initiation
- Environmental improvements
- Monitoring steps

**Example**: "🚜 Cắt bỏ các lá bị bệnh để ngăn chặn lây lan"

### 💚 Health Score
**How healthy**: 0-100% progress bar
- 🟢 80-100%: Healthy
- 🟡 50-80%: Moderate concern
- 🔴 0-50%: Serious issue

**Visual**: `[████████░░] 85%`

### ⚠️ Severity Level
**How serious**: Color-coded badge
- 🟢 MILD - Low priority
- 🟡 MODERATE - Monitor closely
- 🟠 SEVERE - Urgent action needed
- 🔴 CRITICAL - Emergency treatment

### 💊 Treatment Methods
**How to fix**: 3-4 treatment approaches
1. Chemical treatment (with specific products)
2. Organic alternatives
3. Physical removal
4. Environmental controls

**Example**: 
- "🧪 Xử lý hóa học" - Fungicide spray
- "🌿 Xử lý hữu cơ" - Neem oil
- "✂️ Cắt bỏ" - Remove affected leaves
- "☀️ Cải thiện điều kiện" - Increase light

### 🛡️ Prevention Tips
**How to prevent**: 4-5 specific strategies
- Preventive care practices
- Environmental management
- Plant spacing
- Variety selection
- Sanitation practices

**Example**:
```
• Tránh tưới nước vào lá
• Cải thiện thông gió
• Loại bỏ lá bị bệnh
• Khử trùng dụng cụ
• Chọn giống chống bệnh
```
